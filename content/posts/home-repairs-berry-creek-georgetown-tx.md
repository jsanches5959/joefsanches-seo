## 6. Mature oaks, pecans and the leaf-drop cycle

Berry Creek's trees are mature live oaks and pecans, and our tree page puts them in the oak-wilt picture directly: Georgetown's live oaks grow in connected groves - along the San Gabriel River and through Berry Creek and Old Town - so one infected tree can move through a whole street.

- **Timing.** Oak wilt spreads through fresh cuts during the high-risk months. The safe window runs July through January - which means right now, late fall and winter, is when Berry Creek oak work should happen, and every cut on an oak should be painted immediately, in any season. ## What order to do it in

1. **Water and sun first.** Stucco and limestone cracks, open caulk joints and the failing south and west paint - anything letting water in or baking off.
2. **The panel conversation.** Older Berry Creek homes are the ones that most often need the electrical capacity check before new loads get added.
3. **Trees in the safe window.** Oak work between July and January, while the window is open, with cuts painted immediately.
4. **Approvals before crews.** Exterior color changes, structural outdoor work and large tree removals go to the HOA first, so no job stops mid-stream.
5. **Rooms and surfaces last.** The primary bath, the drywall and popcorn work, then floors, then washing as the finishing pass.

## Frequently asked questions

**What breaks first in an established Berry Creek home?**
The finishes, not the structure. Our Georgetown pages name the Berry Creek pattern directly: decades of wear on the original finishes, a settling crack over a doorway, stucco and limestone cracks under failing south and west paint, and primary baths and older floors ready for their second cycle. Handled as one list, most of it is a handyman half-day plus a paint scope, not a renovation.

**How much does it cost to repaint a Berry Creek house?**
Interior rooms run $350 to $650 for a bedroom, walls only, and a whole 2,000 sq ft interior runs $4,000 to $8,000 for walls or $6,500 to $12,000 with ceilings and trim. For exteriors, an Austin-area estimator puts a 2,500 sq ft exterior at about $4,375 to $10,625. Stucco and limestone homes like Berry Creek's need crack repair and the right masonry primer before paint, so exterior pricing is a written estimate after a walk-through rather than a per-foot guess.

**Are the drywall cracks in my Berry Creek home a foundation problem?**
Usually they are settling, and the named Berry Creek example is a crack over a doorway. Cracks that are wide, offset, growing, or paired with doors that suddenly stick deserve a specialist's look first - our drywall cracks guide walks through the difference. A cosmetic repair is cut back to solid material, re-taped, floated, and matched to the existing texture before paint, and popcorn ceilings are removed and retextured as part of the same scope where they are in the way.

**When should I trim my Berry Creek live oaks?**
Between July and January. Oak wilt spreads through fresh pruning cuts most easily in the spring high-risk period, and Berry Creek's oaks grow in connected groves, so one infected tree can move through a whole street. Late fall and winter are the safe working window, trimming runs about $245 to $1,029 depending on size and canopy, and every cut should be painted immediately, in any season.

**Do I need HOA approval for repairs in Berry Creek?**
For like-for-like repairs, generally no. For anything that changes the exterior look or footprint - exterior color changes, a new or rebuilt deck, changing a fence line, removing a large tree - expect a submittal to the HOA first, and large trees can also need a City of Georgetown permit. We flag which applies during the walk-through and get the approval in hand before work starts.
