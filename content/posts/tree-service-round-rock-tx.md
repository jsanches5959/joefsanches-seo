# Tree Service in Round Rock, TX: Removal, Trimming and What It Costs

Round Rock spans older neighborhoods with mature shade trees and newer subdivisions with younger plantings, mostly on the clay soils east of the Balcones Escarpment. That combination creates a few tree problems we see again and again.

## Common Round Rock tree issues

- **Big trees close to the house.** On clay soil, large thirsty trees near the foundation can contribute to foundation movement during drought. See [tree roots and foundation damage](/posts/tree-roots-foundation-damage-texas).
- **Declining fast-growers.** Arizona ash, Bradford pear and other fast growers planted in the 1980s–2000s are reaching the end of their useful life and dropping limbs.
- **Roof and driveway clearance** on mature live oaks and cedar elms. See [live oak trimming](/posts/live-oak-trimming-central-texas).
- **Fence-line hackberries.** See [hackberry removal](/posts/hackberry-tree-removal-texas).
- **Storm damage** from spring winds and occasional ice.

## Rules to check

- **Your HOA.** Many Round Rock neighborhoods require approval for tree removal. See [HOA tree removal rules](/posts/hoa-tree-removal-rules-leander-tx).
- **The city.** Round Rock has tree protection rules, mainly tied to development. Check with the city before removing a very large tree, a tree near the street, or a tree tied to construction work.
- **Austin city limits.** Some addresses near Round Rock are in Austin, which has strict protected-tree rules. See [tree removal permits](/posts/tree-removal-permit-leander-cedar-park-austin).

## Oak wilt

Round Rock's live oaks and red oaks face the same oak wilt risk as the rest of the Austin area. Avoid pruning or wounding oaks from February through June, and paint every cut. See [when to trim oak trees](/posts/when-to-trim-oak-trees-central-texas).

## Bradford pears: a special case

Bradford (Callery) pears were planted widely in Texas subdivisions. They grow fast, but their tight branch angles make them prone to splitting apart in wind and ice, often around 15 to 25 years old. If yours has started splitting, removal is usually the better choice than repeated repairs.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does tree removal cost in Round Rock?**
Roughly $250 to $700 for small trees, $600 to $1,800 for medium, and $1,500 to $4,000 or more for large trees, depending on access and what's underneath.

**Should I remove a tree that's close to my house in Round Rock?**
Not automatically. On clay soil, removing a large established tree can also change soil moisture. If you see signs of foundation movement, have the foundation evaluated first.

**Why do Bradford pear trees split?**
Their branches grow at tight angles with weak attachments, so as they mature they're prone to splitting in wind and ice.
