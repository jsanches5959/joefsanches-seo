# How to Choose a Tree Service in Leander: 10 Questions to Ask First

Tree work is one of the few home services where a mistake can put a tree through your roof. Anyone with a chainsaw and a truck can call themselves a tree service, so it pays to ask a few questions before you hire.

## 10 questions to ask

### 1. Can I get the price in writing?

A real quote lists what's being done, tree by tree, and the total. Verbal prices lead to arguments.

### 2. What exactly is included?

Ask specifically about:

- **haul-off** of all limbs and wood,
- **stump grinding** (usually a separate line),
- **cleanup** (raking and blowing the yard, clearing the roof and gutters).

The cheapest quote often leaves one of these out.

### 3. How will you take the tree down?

For a tree near a house, fence or pool, it should come down in sections, lowered in a controlled way, not just dropped. Ask how they'll protect what's underneath.

### 4. Do you follow oak wilt rules?

For oaks, the answer should be: no pruning or wounding February through June except emergencies, and **every cut gets painted**. If they don't know what you're talking about, find someone else. See [when to trim oak trees](/posts/when-to-trim-oak-trees-central-texas).

### 5. Will you top my tree?

The right answer is no. Topping (cutting a tree's crown back to stubs) damages trees and creates weak, dangerous regrowth.

### 6. Do you use climbing spikes on trees you're trimming?

Spikes are fine on a tree being removed, but they wound living trees. They shouldn't be used to trim a tree you're keeping.

### 7. What happens if something gets damaged?

Ask how they handle damage to your property, and get it in writing. Ask whether they carry liability coverage, and ask to see proof.

### 8. Who will be on the job, and who do I call?

You want one point of contact who answers the phone before, during and after the job.

### 9. Do I need a permit or HOA approval?

A good tree service will flag protected trees and remind you about HOA approval. See [tree removal permits](/posts/tree-removal-permit-leander-cedar-park-austin) and [HOA rules](/posts/hoa-tree-removal-rules-leander-tx).

### 10. When will you start, and how long will it take?

Get a date. For non-emergency work, a reasonable wait is normal. Be wary of anyone who pressures you to decide on the spot.

## Red flags

- **Door-to-door after a storm**, pushing you to sign today.
- **Cash only, full payment upfront.**
- **No written quote.**
- **Recommends topping** a healthy tree.
- **Doesn't know about oak wilt.**
- **Price far below everyone else**, with no explanation of what's included.

## Frequently asked questions

**What should I look for in a tree service?**
A written quote that lists removal, haul-off and stump grinding separately, a clear plan for protecting your property, knowledge of oak wilt rules, and one point of contact.

**Is the cheapest tree service quote the best?**
Not always. Low quotes often leave out haul-off, stump grinding or cleanup. Compare what's included, not just the total.

**Should a tree service top my tree?**
No. Topping damages trees and leads to weak, hazardous regrowth. Proper pruning is selective.

**How do I avoid tree service scams after a storm?**
Get a written quote, don't pay in full upfront, get a local address and phone number, and avoid high-pressure, sign-today offers.
