## 1. Exterior paint and caulk: the first cycle is due

On a 2016 to 2019 Wolf Ranch home, the exterior paint and caulk system is at the end of its first run. The south and west elevations fail first in Central Texas sun, caulk joints open at the trim and around windows, and the stucco and limestone elevations common in Wolf Ranch need crack repair and the right masonry primer before paint, not paint over cracks. Left alone, open joints are how water gets behind the elevation - a paint job becomes a repair job.

The order matters more than the brand: repair first, then caulk, prime and paint. Our [Georgetown painting page](/services/interior-exterior-painting-georgetown-tx) covers what a full repaint includes, and our [exterior paint guide](/posts/exterior-paint-texas-heat-how-long-lasts) covers how long a good job lasts in this heat. One Wolf Ranch note: exterior color changes need HOA approval first - get the approval in hand before the crew, not after.

## 2. River lots: decks, drainage and moisture

The river is why people buy in Wolf Ranch, and the lots that back to the greenbelt carry a specific list. Decks and patios facing the river sit in shade and hold moisture, so boards and joist ends rot earlier than the same structure on an open lot, and limestone patios and pool surrounds get slippery as algae builds. Footings are the other river-lot item: thin soil over limestone means a footing set shallow works loose as the clay swells and shrinks, and a deck that rocks or a gate that will not latch is usually that story, not age.

New decks run $30-$50 per square foot, most projects $3,946-$10,909; repairs run $852-$3,345, and composite is the common pick on shaded river lots because it costs more up front and almost nothing to maintain. Drainage gets one look on every river-lot walk-through - water should move away from the slab toward the greenbelt, not pond against the house after a San Gabriel rain. ## 3. Settling cracks in newer construction

A new subdivision's ground gets graded, filled and built on fast, and the first decade of wet-dry cycles over Georgetown limestone and clay shows up inside as the classic pattern: a diagonal crack over a doorway, a ceiling seam that reappears in the same joint, nail pops along one wall. In a home under 10 years old this is almost always cosmetic settling, not foundation failure - our [drywall cracks guide](/posts/drywall-cracks-when-to-worry-central-texas) walks through which cracks are which.

The repair that lasts is cut back to solid material, re-tape, float, and match the existing texture before paint. Wolf Ranch interiors lean smooth and high-finish, which is less forgiving than orange peel: a patch that skips the texture and sheen match shows every time the afternoon sun comes across the wall. Details are on the [Georgetown drywall page](/services/drywall-repair-georgetown-tx).

## 4. The warranty clock: the earliest homes are at the line

The national builders in Wolf Ranch carried the standard structure: one year on workmanship, two on systems, ten on structure. The earliest homes, built in 2016, reach the end of that 10-year structural window right about now - and anything that looks structural - cracks that are wide, offset, or growing, doors that suddenly stick - is worth a specialist's look while you still know which side of the warranty line you are on. In the sections still building out, the move runs the other way: build the punch list before each window closes and get it in front of the builder while it is still their bill. Once the warranty is gone, the small stuff becomes a handyman list. ## 6. Oaks, fences and the wash

Wolf Ranch's oaks - the planted street trees and the mature canopy along the river - run on Central Texas rules:

- **Timing.** Oak wilt spreads through fresh cuts during the high-risk months. The safe window runs July through January - which means right now, late fall and winter, is when Wolf Ranch oak work should happen. ## What order to do it in

1. **Water first.** Caulk, open stucco and limestone cracks, drainage running toward the house - anything letting water in outranks everything cosmetic.
2. **Warranty-window items.** The earliest Wolf Ranch homes are at the 10-year structural line now; anything that might be the builder's bill gets listed and checked before its window closes.
3. **Trees in the safe window.** Oak work between July and January, while the window is open.
4. **Surfaces.** Paint, then floors if the builder carpet is going, then washing as the finishing pass.

## Frequently asked questions

**What breaks first in a 2016 to 2019 Wolf Ranch home?**
The exterior paint and caulk system goes first, and the south and west elevations show it earliest. After that it is the wear items on the same clock: builder carpet, fence pickets and gates, the small settling cracks over doorways, and - on river lots - deck boards and joist ends that sit in shade and hold moisture. The structure itself, on a maintained home, is rarely the problem.

**How much does it cost to repaint a Wolf Ranch house?**
Interior rooms run $350 to $650 for a bedroom, walls only, and a whole 2,000 sq ft interior runs $4,000 to $8,000 for walls or $6,500 to $12,000 with ceilings and trim. Exterior pricing depends on size, stories, elevation materials and how much caulk and crack repair the prep uncovers, so it is quoted as a written estimate after a walk-through rather than a per-foot guess. Exterior color changes also need HOA approval in Wolf Ranch.

**Are the drywall cracks in my Wolf Ranch home a foundation problem?**
Usually not. Newer lots over limestone and clay move slightly with every wet and dry cycle, and diagonal cracks over doorways and reappearing ceiling seams are the normal signature of that movement. Cracks that are wide, growing fast, offset from one side to the other, or paired with doors suddenly sticking deserve a foundation specialist's look before any cosmetic repair - and on a 2016 home, check the builder's 10-year structural warranty line first, because that window is closing now.

**When should I trim my Wolf Ranch live oaks?**
Between July and January. Oak wilt spreads through fresh pruning cuts most easily in the spring high-risk period, so late fall and winter are the safe working window in Central Texas. Trimming runs about $245 to $1,029 depending on size and canopy, and cuts should be painted immediately as part of the job.

**Do I need HOA approval for repairs in Wolf Ranch?**
For like-for-like repairs, generally no. For anything that changes the exterior look or footprint - exterior color changes, a new or rebuilt deck, changing a fence line, removing a large tree - expect a submittal to the Wolf Ranch HOA first, and large trees can also need a City of Georgetown permit. We flag which applies during the walk-through and get the approval in hand before work starts.
