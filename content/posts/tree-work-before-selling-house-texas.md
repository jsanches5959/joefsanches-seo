# Tree Work Before Selling Your House: What Buyers and Inspectors Notice

Trees can help sell a house or give a buyer something to negotiate with. A mature live oak framing the front yard is a selling point. The same tree with limbs on the roof and dead branches over the driveway becomes an inspection item.

## What inspectors often flag

- **Limbs touching or overhanging the roof:** risk of roof damage and easy pest access.
- **Dead trees or large dead limbs** near the house, driveway or play areas.
- **Trees very close to the foundation**, especially on clay soils. See [tree roots and foundation damage](/posts/tree-roots-foundation-damage-texas).
- **Roots lifting sidewalks or driveways.**
- **Trees touching the service line** to the house. See [trees near power lines](/posts/tree-near-power-lines-texas).

Each of these can turn into a repair request or a price reduction. Dealing with them before listing usually costs less than negotiating over them later.

## What makes listing photos look better

- **Raise the canopy** on front-yard trees so the house isn't hidden.
- **Thin dense trees** to let light reach the lawn and the front of the house.
- **Remove dead and dying trees** and overgrown shrubs.
- **Clean up fence lines** where volunteer trees and vines have taken over.
- **Grind leftover stumps** in the lawn.

Combine it with pressure washing the driveway and sidewalks, and curb appeal improves a lot for a modest cost.

## What usually isn't worth it

- **Removing healthy, well-placed shade trees.** Mature trees are an asset.
- **Big landscaping overhauls** right before listing, unless the yard is truly a problem.

## Timing

- Schedule tree work **a few weeks before listing photos**, so the yard has time to recover and debris is gone.
- If your trees are oaks, remember **oak wilt season (February through June)**. If you're listing in spring, plan oak trimming for winter. See [when to trim oak trees](/posts/when-to-trim-oak-trees-central-texas).

## Buying instead?

If an inspection flagged trees, get a written quote for the work before closing. It helps with negotiation, and you'll know exactly what you're taking on.

## Frequently asked questions

**Should I trim trees before selling my house?**
Yes, if limbs are on the roof, there's deadwood over the house or driveway, or trees are hiding the front of the house. It improves photos and heads off inspection requests.

**Do overhanging branches hurt a home inspection?**
Inspectors commonly note branches touching or overhanging the roof, which can lead to repair requests from buyers.

**Should I remove a big tree before selling?**
Usually not, if it's healthy and well-placed. Mature shade trees are generally an asset. Remove dead, dangerous or badly placed trees.
