# How Much Does Tree Removal Cost in Leander, TX? (2026 Guide)

Most people searching this have one of two trees in mind: a dead one they've been worried about since the last storm, or a healthy one that's simply in the wrong place. Either way, the first question is the same: what's this going to cost?

The honest answer is that a tree removal price depends far more on **where the tree is** than on the tree itself. A 40-foot cedar elm in an open backyard and the same tree leaning over a roof are two completely different jobs.

Here's how pricing works around Leander, Cedar Park, Georgetown and the north Austin suburbs.

## Ballpark ranges

These are rough planning ranges, not quotes. Your price depends on the specific tree and site.

| Tree size | Typical range |
|---|---|
| Small (under about 25 ft), easy access | $250 – $700 |
| Medium (25–50 ft) | $600 – $1,800 |
| Large (50 ft+) or a big live oak | $1,500 – $4,000+ |
| Stump grinding (per stump) | $100 – $400 |
| Emergency / storm work | Often 1.5–2× normal rates |

If a quote is far below these numbers, ask what's included. Haul-off and stump grinding are the two items most often left off a low bid.

## What actually drives the price

### 1. What's underneath it

If the tree can be dropped in one piece into open yard, it's quick. If it's over a house, a fence, a pool or a neighbor's yard, it has to come down in sections, often roped down piece by piece. That takes more time, more people and more skill, and it's the biggest single factor in price.

### 2. Access

Can equipment get to it? A backyard behind a narrow side gate means everything gets carried out by hand. Leander's newer neighborhoods, with tight side yards and fences on every property line, often fall into this category.

### 3. Tree species and wood

Live oaks are dense, heavy and spread wide, so they take longer than their height suggests. Cedar (Ashe juniper) is lighter but often grows in thick clumps. Hackberry and Arizona ash are softer and usually faster.

### 4. Condition

A dead or storm-damaged tree can be more expensive, not less. Dead wood is brittle and unpredictable, so the crew has to work more carefully.

### 5. Cleanup and haul-off

Hauling away the trunk and limbs is real labor and dump fees. Some homeowners keep the firewood to save money. Make sure your quote says which you're getting.

### 6. The stump

Removal usually means cutting the trunk close to the ground. Grinding the stump below grade is a separate line item. See our guide to [stump grinding costs](/posts/stump-grinding-cost-leander-cedar-park).

## How to get an accurate quote fast

1. **Take three photos:** the whole tree, the base of the trunk, and what's under or around it (house, fence, lines).
2. **Note the rough height**, and whether the tree is dead, leaning or damaged.
3. **Send them with the estimate form** at the bottom of this page. Most jobs can be roughly priced from photos, and bigger ones get a quick walk-through.

A written quote should list removal, haul-off and stump grinding separately so you can choose.

## Things that can save you money

- **Bundle work.** Removing one tree and trimming two others in one visit costs less than three separate trips.
- **Keep the wood.** If you have a fireplace, keeping the firewood can trim the haul-off cost.
- **Skip stump grinding** if the spot is in a natural area where a low-cut stump won't bother you.
- **Don't wait for an emergency.** A dead tree removed on your schedule costs less than the same tree after it's split in a storm.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does it cost to remove a large live oak in Leander?**
Large live oaks are among the more expensive removals, often $1,500 to $4,000 or more, because they're heavy and spread wide over houses and fences. A photo of the whole tree and what's under it is enough to get a starting range.

**Is stump grinding included in tree removal?**
Usually not. Most tree services quote it separately, typically $100 to $400 per stump depending on size. Ask for it as its own line on the quote.

**Does homeowners insurance pay for tree removal?**
Sometimes. If a tree falls on your house or another covered structure, many policies help pay to remove it. A dead tree that's still standing is usually your cost. See [tree fell on my house: who pays?](/posts/tree-fell-on-house-texas-insurance)

**Is it cheaper to remove a tree in winter?**
Scheduling is often easier in the cooler months, and you'll have better visibility of the branch structure once leaves drop on deciduous trees. Price differences are usually modest; access and location matter far more.
