## 3. Sun-beaten elevations: exterior paint and caulk

In Round Rock that means sun-beaten south and west elevations in Forest Creek and Cat Hollow, and outside, those elevations fail first in Central Texas sun while caulk joints open at the trim and around windows - left alone, open joints are how water gets behind the elevation.

The order matters more than the brand: repair first, then caulk, prime and paint. ## 5. Live oaks, leaf drop, decks and drainage

Grounds work in Cat Hollow starts with the trees: keeping up with live oak leaf drop in Cat Hollow is the recurring item our [Round Rock landscaping page](/services/landscaping-grounds-maintenance-round-rock-tx) names, and fall is the working season - leaf and debris cleanup, a fresh mulch layer before winter, and oak trimming inside the safe window. The oaks run on Central Texas rules:

- **Timing.** Oak wilt spreads through fresh cuts during the high-risk months. The safe window runs July through January - which means right now, late fall and winter, is when oak work should happen. November through January is the heart of that window. Our [live oak trimming guide](/posts/live-oak-trimming-central-texas) covers raising, thinning and reducing, and what never to do to an oak.
- **Removal is the last resort, priced by size.** Removal in the Austin area averages about $736, with most jobs between $256 and $1,962 depending on height and access. Round Rock protects its largest trees: designated monarch trees - a live oak around 36 inches or more in diameter, a pecan around 34 inches, a cedar elm around 25 inches - cannot be removed without city approval, and HOAs in neighborhoods like Cat Hollow may also require approval first. The [Round Rock tree page](/services/tree-removal-round-rock-tx) has the full breakdown.

The ground itself is the other outdoor item. Central Texas clay swells and shrinks with the weather and works footings loose, so footings have to be set right or a deck works loose with the seasons. New decks run $30-$50 per square foot, most projects $3,946-$10,909; repairs run $852-$3,345. See the [Round Rock deck and patio page](/services/deck-patio-builder-round-rock-tx) for how outdoor builds are scoped, including when an HOA submittal or a City of Round Rock permit applies. Drainage gets one look on every walk-through: water should move away from the slab, not pond against the house after a heavy rain.

## What order to do it in

1. **Water first.** Caulk, open joints, drainage running toward the house - anything letting water in outranks everything cosmetic.
2. **Trees in the safe window.** Oak work between July and January, while the window is open - the leaf drop and canopy do not get smaller for waiting.
3. **The accumulated list.** The handyman half-day that clears years of small deferred items in one visit.
4. **Surfaces.** Paint, then floors if the carpet is going, then the kitchen scope, then washing as the finishing pass.

The finishes, in a predictable order: caulk joints opening at trim and windows on the sun-beaten elevations, hardware and latches wearing out, and carpet wearing out on the traffic lines. It is wear on the original finishes, not a failing structure - on a maintained home, the structure itself is rarely the problem.

**How much does it cost to repaint a Cat Hollow house?**
Interior rooms run $350 to $650 for a bedroom, walls only, and a whole 2,000 sq ft interior runs $4,000 to $8,000 for walls or $6,500 to $12,000 with ceilings and trim. Exterior pricing depends on size, stories, elevation materials and how much caulk and crack repair the prep uncovers on those sun-beaten south and west elevations, so it is quoted as a written estimate after a walk-through rather than a per-foot guess.

**When should I trim my Cat Hollow oaks?**
Between July and January. Oak wilt spreads through fresh pruning cuts most easily in the spring high-risk period, so late fall and winter are the safe working window in Central Texas, and November through January is the heart of it. Trimming runs about $245 to $1,029 depending on size and canopy, and cuts should be painted immediately as part of the job.
