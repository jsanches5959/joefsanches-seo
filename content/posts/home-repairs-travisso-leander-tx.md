# Home Repairs in Travisso, Leander TX: The First-Decade List for Newer Luxury Homes

Travisso sits on the Balcones Escarpment on the Cedar Park side of the Leander line, in Leander ISD, built by Toll Brothers, Taylor Morrison and Highland Homes starting in the early 2010s. The draw is the terrain: ridgelines, live oak canyons, resort pool and clubhouse, and sunset views that most of Leander cannot offer. Homes here run from the $500,000s to $1.2M and up, and the community holds them to a premium standard.

Newer does not mean maintenance-free. The earliest Travisso homes are now 10 to 14 years old, which is exactly when the first cycle lands: builder paint and caulk reach the end of their life in Texas sun, the 10-year structural warranty window closes on the oldest homes, and the hill country ground under those view lots has had a decade of wet-dry cycles to move. This is the Travisso list we see, what each item costs in the area, and the order to tackle it in.

## 1. Exterior caulk and paint: the first cycle is due

Builder-grade exterior paint on a 2013 to 2016 Travisso home has now had a decade or more of west-facing Texas sun, and the elevated lots take more of it than flat ones. The failure pattern is the same as in older neighborhoods, just earlier in the home's life: caulk joints opening at the trim and around windows, fading and chalking on the south and west walls, then water finding the gaps. On stucco and stone elevations, the caulk lines are the whole game - the wall materials last, the sealant does not.

The order matters more than the brand of paint. Repair any siding or drywall damage first, re-caulk every joint, prime the bare spots, then two coats. Our [exterior paint guide](/posts/exterior-paint-texas-heat-how-long-lasts) covers how long a good job lasts in this heat, and the [Leander painting page](/services/interior-exterior-painting-leander-tx) covers what a full repaint includes. One Travisso note: exterior color changes need HOA approval first, and this HOA enforces consistently - get the approval in hand before the crew, not after.

## 2. Settling cracks on escarpment lots

Travisso's views come from elevation, and elevation means cut-and-fill lots over limestone and clay. A decade of wet-dry cycles shows up inside as the classic pattern: a diagonal crack over a doorway, a ceiling seam that reappears in the same joint, nail pops along one wall. In a 10-year-old home this is almost always cosmetic settling, not foundation failure - our [drywall cracks guide](/posts/drywall-cracks-when-to-worry-central-texas) walks through which cracks are which.

The repair that lasts is cut back to solid material, re-tape, float, and match the existing texture before paint. Travisso interiors lean smooth and high-finish, which is less forgiving than orange peel: a patch that skips the texture and sheen match shows every time the afternoon sun comes across the wall. Details are on the [Leander drywall repair page](/services/drywall-repair-leander-tx).

## 3. The warranty clock: what changes at year 10

Most Travisso builders carried the standard structure: one year on workmanship, two on systems, ten on structure. For the earliest homes, that 10-year structural coverage is expiring now or already has. Two practical moves follow from that. First, anything that looks structural - cracks that are wide, offset, or growing, doors that suddenly stick, slope or drainage changes against the foundation - is worth a specialist's look while you still know which side of the warranty line you are on. Second, once the warranty is gone, the punch list is yours: the small stuff the builder's warranty department used to absorb (sticky latches, grout cracks, trim gaps, settling nail pops) becomes a handyman list. Our [Leander handyman page](/services/handyman-services-leander-tx) covers how that work is priced - by the job, in half-day blocks, not by the hour.

## 4. Decks, patios and the view lots

The outdoor living space is half the reason people pay the Travisso premium, and it is also where the terrain bills come due. Sloped view lots mean deeper footings, retaining edges, and drainage that has to move storm water away from the house and off the slope. Original builder decks on the earliest homes are hitting the same end-of-life pattern as everywhere else - sun-split boards, fasteners working loose as the ground moves - with one addition: a deck that fails on a view lot takes the view with it for a season.

New builds run $30-$50 per square foot installed (most projects $3,946-$10,909); repairs typically run $852-$3,345. A sound frame can be resurfaced; failed framing or footings are a rebuild. The [Leander deck and patio page](/services/deck-patio-builder-leander-tx) covers materials and the permit step - and in Travisso, expect the HOA submittal to come before the city step, not after.

## 5. The oaks: young canopy, same rules

Travisso's live oak canyons are protected character, and the oaks planted at build are now real canopy. The rules do not change because the trees are younger:

- **Timing.** Oak wilt spreads through fresh cuts during the high-risk months. The safe window runs July through January - which means right now, late fall and winter, is when Travisso oak work should happen. Our [live oak trimming guide](/posts/live-oak-trimming-central-texas) covers raising, thinning and reducing, and what never to do to an oak.
- **Removal is the last resort, priced by size.** Removal in the Austin area averages about $736, with most jobs between $256 and $1,962 depending on height and access - canyon and gated-section lots with no alley access sit at the top of the range. The [Leander tree page](/tree-removal-leander-tx) has the full breakdown.

Check your section's HOA rules before removing any mature oak; Travisso's standards are enforced, and a quick check beats a violation letter.

## 6. Pressure washing and the stone-and-stucco reset

Limestone dust, hard-water spotting on stone, and mildew on shaded canyon-side walls build up slowly enough that owners stop seeing them - until the neighbor washes theirs. A driveway runs $100-$250 and a full house wash $186-$459, most homes around $300. Soft washing for stucco and siding, higher pressure for concrete. On a premium street, it is the cheapest way to make a 2014 house read as maintained. The [Leander pressure washing page](/services/pressure-washing-leander-tx) has the details.

## What order to do it in

1. **Water first.** Caulk, paint failure, drainage running toward the house - anything letting water in outranks everything cosmetic.
2. **Warranty-window items.** Anything that might be structural gets checked while the oldest homes still know their warranty status.
3. **Trees in the safe window.** Oak work between July and January, while the window is open.
4. **Surfaces.** Paint, then floors if the builder carpet is going, then washing as the finishing pass.

## Frequently asked questions

**What breaks first in a 10-year-old Travisso home?**
The exterior caulk and paint system goes first - joints open and west walls chalk and fade after about a decade of Texas sun on an elevated lot. After that it is the wear items on the same clock: builder carpet, deck boards and fasteners, and the small settling cracks over doorways. The structure itself, on a maintained home, is rarely the problem.

**How much does it cost to repaint a Travisso house?**
Interior rooms run $350 to $650 for a bedroom, walls only, and a whole 2,000 sq ft interior runs $4,000 to $8,000 for walls or $6,500 to $12,000 with ceilings and trim. Exterior pricing depends on size, stories, elevation materials and how much caulk and repair work the prep uncovers, so it is quoted as a written estimate after a walk-through rather than a per-foot guess. Exterior color changes also need HOA approval in Travisso.

**Are the drywall cracks in my Travisso home a foundation problem?**
Usually not. Escarpment lots over limestone and clay move slightly with every wet and dry cycle, and diagonal cracks over doorways and reappearing ceiling seams are the normal signature of that movement. Cracks that are wide, growing fast, offset from one side to the other, or paired with doors suddenly sticking deserve a foundation specialist's look before any cosmetic repair - and on the oldest Travisso homes, check whether the 10-year structural warranty still applies before you pay for that look yourself.

**When should I trim my Travisso live oaks?**
Between July and January. Oak wilt spreads through fresh pruning cuts most easily in the spring high-risk period, so late fall and winter are the safe working window in Central Texas. Trimming runs about $245 to $1,029 depending on size and canopy, and cuts should be painted immediately as part of the job.

**Do I need HOA approval for repairs in Travisso?**
For like-for-like repairs, generally no. For anything that changes the exterior look or footprint - a new or rebuilt deck, a patio cover, exterior color changes, removing a mature tree - expect a submittal to the Travisso HOA first. Travisso enforces its standards consistently, so we flag which applies during the walk-through and get the approval in hand before work starts.
