## 1. Decades of wear on the original finishes: the handyman list

Round Rock homes accumulate the same kind of list everywhere - a door that will not latch, a bathroom fan that rattles, baseboard that came loose, three things that need mounting. ## 2. Ceiling stains and settling cracks: drywall after the leak is fixed

Most drywall calls in Round Rock are small: a doorknob through a bedroom wall in Round Rock West, a settling crack over a doorway in Brushy Creek, a ceiling stain after a roof leak in Forest Creek. The order matters on the Forest Creek version: the leak source gets fixed first, the stained material gets cut back to solid material once it is dry, and only then does the patch go in - a repair floated over a still-active leak comes right back. Those repairs are easy to do and easy to do badly - a patch that does not match the surrounding texture is more obvious than the hole was, so texture is matched before finishing, so the repair disappears into the wall. Our [drywall cracks guide](/posts/drywall-cracks-when-to-worry-central-texas) walks through which cracks are cosmetic settling and which ones earn a specialist's look first: the wide ones, the fast-growing ones, the ones offset from one side to the other, and the ones paired with doors that suddenly stick. Details are on the [Round Rock drywall page](/services/drywall-repair-round-rock-tx).

## 3. Sun-beaten elevations: exterior paint and caulk

Painting is mostly preparation. The visible part - rolling color on a wall - is the fast part; what separates a paint job that looks good for a decade from one that peels in two years is what happens before the first coat: patching, caulking, sanding, priming and masking. In Round Rock that means sun-beaten south and west elevations in Forest Creek and Cat Hollow, and outside, those elevations fail first in Central Texas sun while caulk joints open at the trim and around windows - left alone, open joints are how water gets behind the elevation.

The order matters more than the brand: repair first, then caulk, prime and paint. Our [Round Rock painting page](/services/interior-exterior-painting-round-rock-tx) covers what a full repaint includes, and our [exterior paint guide](/posts/exterior-paint-texas-heat-how-long-lasts) covers how long a good job lasts in this heat. One Forest Creek note: an exterior color change needs HOA architectural approval first, even when the new color is close to the old one - get the approval in hand before the crew, not after. A repaint in the same color usually does not need it.

## 5. Mature trees, decks and drainage on black clay

Round Rock's trees split along I-35, and Forest Creek's canopy is the established side of that split: mature trees over 1990s homes, now big enough that the scope is real canopy work, not a street-tree trim. The oaks among them run on Central Texas rules:

- **Timing.** Oak wilt spreads through fresh cuts during the high-risk months. The safe window runs July through January - which means right now, late fall and winter, is when oak work should happen. November through January is the heart of that window. Our [live oak trimming guide](/posts/live-oak-trimming-central-texas) covers raising, thinning and reducing, and what never to do to an oak.
- **Removal is the last resort, priced by size.** Removal in the Austin area averages about $736, with most jobs between $256 and $1,962 depending on height and access. Round Rock protects its largest trees: designated monarch trees - a live oak around 36 inches or more in diameter, a pecan around 34 inches, a cedar elm around 25 inches - cannot be removed without city approval, and HOAs in neighborhoods like Forest Creek may also require approval first. The [Round Rock tree page](/services/tree-removal-round-rock-tx) has the full breakdown.

The ground itself is the other outdoor item. The black clay under Brushy Creek and Forest Creek swells and shrinks with the weather and works footings loose, so footings have to be set right or a deck works loose with the seasons. New decks run $30-$50 per square foot, most projects $3,946-$10,909; repairs run $852-$3,345. See the [Round Rock deck and patio page](/services/deck-patio-builder-round-rock-tx) for how outdoor builds are scoped, including when an HOA submittal or a City of Round Rock permit applies. Drainage gets one look on every walk-through: water should move away from the slab, not pond against the house after a heavy rain.

## What order to do it in

1. **Water first.** The roof leak behind the ceiling stain, caulk, open joints, drainage running toward the house - anything letting water in outranks everything cosmetic.
2. **Trees in the safe window.** Oak work between July and January, while the window is open - the mature canopy does not get smaller for waiting.
3. **The accumulated list.** The handyman half-day that clears years of small deferred items in one visit.
4. **Surfaces.** Paint, then floors if the builder carpet is going, then the kitchen scope, then washing as the finishing pass.

## Frequently asked questions

**What breaks first in a 1990s Forest Creek home?**
The finishes, in a predictable order: caulk joints opening at trim and windows, hardware and latches wearing out, ceiling stains where a roof leak got ahead of a repair, and builder carpet wearing out on the traffic lines. It is decades of wear on the original finishes, not a failing structure - on a maintained home, the structure itself is rarely the problem.

**How much does it cost to repaint a Forest Creek house?**
Interior rooms run $350 to $650 for a bedroom, walls only, and a whole 2,000 sq ft interior runs $4,000 to $8,000 for walls or $6,500 to $12,000 with ceilings and trim. Exterior pricing depends on size, stories, elevation materials and how much caulk and crack repair the prep uncovers on those sun-beaten south and west elevations, so it is quoted as a written estimate after a walk-through rather than a per-foot guess.

**Should I repair a ceiling stain right away?**
Fix the leak first. A ceiling stain after a roof leak in Forest Creek means water already came through - patching over an active leak guarantees the stain returns. Once the source is fixed and the material is dry, the stained section is cut back to solid material, re-taped, floated and texture-matched so the repair disappears. A stain that keeps growing, or drywall that feels soft, gets opened up rather than covered.

**When should I trim my Forest Creek oaks?**
Between July and January. Oak wilt spreads through fresh pruning cuts most easily in the spring high-risk period, so late fall and winter are the safe working window in Central Texas, and November through January is the heart of it. Trimming runs about $245 to $1,029 depending on size and canopy, and cuts should be painted immediately as part of the job.

**Does Forest Creek have an HOA I need approval from for repairs?**
For like-for-like repairs, generally no approval is needed. An exterior color change needs HOA architectural approval before work starts, even when the new color is close to the old one, and exterior changes, additions and anything structural outdoors usually need an HOA architectural submittal first, with larger or elevated builds sometimes needing a City of Round Rock permit as well. Removing a large tree can need city monarch-tree approval and HOA approval. We flag which applies during the walk-through and get any needed approval in hand before work starts.
