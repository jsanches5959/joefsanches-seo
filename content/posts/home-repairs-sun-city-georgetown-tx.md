# Home Repairs in Sun City Texas, Georgetown TX: The Low-Maintenance List for One-Storey Homes

Sun City Texas is Georgetown's 55+ active adult community, and its homes were designed around a simple idea: one storey, no stairs, everything reachable. That design changes the repair list. The work that matters here is less about square footage and more about how the house gets used every day - a tub that is getting harder to step over, carpet that catches a walker, a yard that takes more weekends than it should, an exterior that has to stay inside the community's approved palette.

## 1. The bathroom: the highest-value room in the house

The most common scope we price in Sun City is the primary bathroom, and it is not cosmetic. A tub-to-shower conversion with a walk-in entry, grab bars placed where they actually get used, wider clearances and better lighting turns the riskiest room in the house into the easiest one. Our [Georgetown remodeling page](/services/home-remodeling-georgetown-tx) covers how that work is scoped - every accessibility item spelled out in writing before demo starts, not discovered mid-project.

The small version of the same idea is a handyman list: grab bars in the shower and at the toilet, lever handles in place of knobs, a bathroom fan that rattles, a door that will not latch. Individually none of it is worth a service call; together it is a half day. Our [Georgetown handyman page](/services/handyman-services-georgetown-tx) covers how that work is priced - by the job, in half-day blocks, not by the hour.

## 2. One floor, no thresholds

Original carpet in a one-storey Sun City floor plan wears out on the traffic lines first, and carpet is the hardest surface in the house on a walker, a cane or a rolling cart. The common upgrade is LVP run through the whole main floor: $3 to $8 per square foot installed, with the average Austin flooring project at $1,595 to $5,069. It is waterproof, forgiving underfoot, and it can run through the plan with flush transitions - no lips at the bathroom or laundry door. The prep point is the slab: a telegraphed dip shows up in plank faster than it ever did in carpet, so levelling is checked before the quote, not after. The [Georgetown flooring page](/services/flooring-installation-georgetown-tx) covers materials and what the install includes.

## 3. Exterior paint, inside the approved palette

Sun City enforces its approved exterior palette closely, and a color change needs HOA architectural approval before work starts, even when the new color is close to the old one. A repaint in the same color usually does not. Get the approval in hand first so the job is not stopped mid-prep - we can paint to an approved palette and give you the color names and product details the HOA asks for.

The houses themselves are stucco and limestone heavy, which means crack repair and the right masonry primer before paint, and the south and west elevations fail first in Central Texas sun. A single-storey exterior on an average Georgetown home typically runs $4,000-$8,000 depending on siding condition and how much caulking and trim repair the prep uncovers. The order matters more than the brand: repair first, then caulk, prime and paint. Our [Georgetown painting page](/services/interior-exterior-painting-georgetown-tx) and our [exterior paint guide](/posts/exterior-paint-texas-heat-how-long-lasts) cover what a full repaint includes and how long a good job lasts in this heat.

## 4. The yard: low maintenance is the whole point

A Sun City yard should not be a second job. The grounds work we scope most often here is subtraction: thinning overgrown beds, swapping thirsty plantings for drought-tolerant, deer-resistant ones that suit Georgetown's limestone soil, refreshing rock and mulch, and putting the yard on a light recurring cycle so it stays inside HOA front-yard standards without weekly effort. Georgetown yards sit on limestone and clay, so beds wash out and mulch thins fast - fall is the working season: leaf and debris cleanup and a fresh mulch layer before winter. Our [Georgetown landscaping page](/services/landscaping-grounds-maintenance-georgetown-tx) covers one-time cleanups and recurring cycles, and we flag anything that needs an HOA architectural submittal before work starts.

## 5. Patios, pool surrounds and the wash

Outdoor living space does the heavy lifting in Sun City, and limestone patios and pool surrounds get slippery as algae builds in the shade. Pressure washing is the cheapest reset there is: a driveway runs $100-$250 and a full house wash $186-$459, most homes around $300, and driveway algae, mildew on siding and dirty walkways are among the most common exterior-maintenance notices in the community - a wash is normally all it takes to close one out. The [Georgetown pressure washing page](/services/pressure-washing-georgetown-tx) has the details.

Where a patio or deck is going in new, the ground is the decision: thin soil over limestone means footings have to be set right or the structure works loose as the clay swells and shrinks, and composite is the common pick here because it costs more up front and almost nothing to maintain. New decks run $30-$50 per square foot, most projects $3,946-$10,909; repairs run $852-$3,345. Structural outdoor work needs an HOA submittal first, and larger or elevated builds can need a City of Georgetown permit - we tell you which applies before work starts. ## 6. Oaks, cracks and the small list

Sun City's oaks are mature now, and mature oaks run on the same rules as everywhere in Central Texas:

- **Timing.** Oak wilt spreads through fresh cuts during the high-risk months. The safe window runs July through January - which means right now, late fall and winter, is when Sun City oak work should happen. Our [live oak trimming guide](/posts/live-oak-trimming-central-texas) covers raising, thinning and reducing, and what never to do to an oak.
- **Removal is the last resort, priced by size.** Removal in the Austin area averages about $736, with most jobs between $256 and $1,962 depending on height and access, and a mature street tree can need HOA approval before it comes down. The [Georgetown tree page](/services/tree-removal-georgetown-tx) has the full breakdown.

Inside, the calls are small and familiar: a doorknob through a bedroom wall, a settling crack over a doorway, a ceiling stain after a roof leak. Those repairs are easy to do and easy to do badly - a patch that does not match the surrounding texture is more obvious than the hole was, so texture gets matched before the finish. Our [drywall cracks guide](/posts/drywall-cracks-when-to-worry-central-texas) walks through which cracks are cosmetic and which deserve a specialist's look first, and the [Georgetown drywall page](/services/drywall-repair-georgetown-tx) covers the repair itself.

## What order to do it in

1. **Safety first.** The bathroom conversion, grab bars and anything slippery underfoot outrank everything cosmetic.
2. **Water and sun.** Caulk, stucco and limestone cracks, and the failing south and west paint - anything letting water in or baking off.
3. **Trees in the safe window.** Oak work between July and January, while the window is open.
4. **Approvals before crews.** Palette, fence, patio and tree submittals go to the HOA first, so no job stops mid-stream.
5. **Surfaces last.** Floors, then washing as the finishing pass.
