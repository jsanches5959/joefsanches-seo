# Google Business Profile Kit: Sanches Group

**Goal:** Show up in the map results for "tree removal near me", "drywall repair Leander" and the rest, and turn those views into calls.

For local trade searches, Google mostly shows the map listings above the websites. The profile, its categories and its reviews do more for those searches than any page on the site.

---

### 1. Business name
Use the legal or DBA name exactly: **Sanches Group** (or **Joe Sanches LLC** if that is what the state filing and signage say). Don't add keywords like "Sanches Group Tree Removal Leander" to the name. Google suspends profiles for that.

### 2. Categories
- **Primary:** General contractor
- **Secondary (add all that apply):** Tree service · Drywall contractor · Painter · Handyman · Landscaper · Pressure washing service · Janitorial service

The primary category carries the most weight. If tree work becomes most of the business, make **Tree service** primary.

### 3. Business description (paste as-is, 750 characters max)
> Sanches Group is a veteran-owned property services company based in Leander, Texas. One company handles the whole property: tree removal, trimming and stump grinding; landscaping and grounds maintenance; remodeling, drywall, painting, flooring and handyman repairs; plus pressure washing, make-readies and facilities maintenance for commercial clients. The company carries founder Joe Sanches's name, so every job has one accountable name on it. Written quotes, full cleanup and one point of contact from estimate to final walkthrough. We work for homeowners, HOAs, property managers, builders, landscape companies and public agencies. Texas HUB certified, SDVOSB and SAM.gov registered. Serving Leander, Cedar Park, Georgetown, Round Rock and the Austin area.

### 4. Services to list (Services tab)
Tree removal · Tree trimming · Stump grinding · Storm cleanup · Lot & brush clearing · Landscaping · Grounds maintenance · Drywall repair · Interior painting · Exterior painting · Remodeling · Flooring · Handyman · Pressure washing · Make-ready / unit turns · Janitorial

### 5. Service areas
Leander · Cedar Park · Georgetown · Liberty Hill · Round Rock · Hutto · Pflugerville · Austin

### 6. Website links
- Main website button: `https://joefsanches.com`
- If Google offers a separate "appointment" or "quote" link, use the tree page: `https://joefsanches.com/trees`

### 7. Photos (this matters more than people think)
Upload 10 or more real job photos: before/after tree removals, a patched-and-painted wall, a crew with a truck, a clean yard after a job. Real photos beat stock photos every time. Add 2–3 new ones every week.

### 8. First three posts
1. **Trees:** a photo of a recent removal. "Joe does trees. Removals, trimming and stump grinding across Leander and Cedar Park, with written quotes and every limb hauled away. Text a photo for a free quote." Button: *Get quote* → `https://joefsanches.com/trees`
2. **Oak wilt:** "Planning to trim your oaks? In Central Texas the safe window is July through January. We paint every oak cut, every time." Button: *Learn more* → `https://joefsanches.com/trees`
3. **Interior work:** a before/after drywall or paint photo. "Joe does drywall, too. Texture-matched patches that disappear into the wall." Button: *Learn more* → `https://joefsanches.com/services/drywall-repair-leander-tx`

### 9. Review request (send by text after every finished job)
> "Hi [name], it's Joe with Sanches Group. Thanks again for having us out. If you were happy with the [tree work / repair], would you leave a quick Google review? It helps a small veteran-owned company more than you'd think: [YOUR REVIEW LINK]"

Ask the same day the job is finished. That's when people are most likely to do it. Reply to every review, good or bad, within a day.

To find your review link: Google Business Profile → **Ask for reviews** → copy link.

---

### Real estate
Joe's real estate work should **not** go on this profile. A real estate agent profile is a different business type, and mixing it in muddies both. If Joe wants map visibility for real estate, set up a separate individual-practitioner profile under his brokerage, following the brokerage's rules.
