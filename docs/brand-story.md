# Brand story: "Joe does ___."

How Sanches Group's very different services fit under one name, and how to write about them so nobody thinks they called the wrong company.

## The idea

The company is **Joe Sanches LLC, doing business as Sanches Group**. The founder's name on the company is the point: it's one name accountable for the work, whatever the work is.

The line that carries it is **"Joe does ___."**

- Joe does trees.
- Joe does drywall.
- Joe does paint.
- Joe does make-readies.
- **Joe does the whole property.** (This is the line the others build to.)

The pattern tells a homeowner who searched for tree removal and one who searched for drywall repair that they both landed in the right place. It also turns a long service list into one simple idea instead of a scattered set of offerings.

"Joe" means the company, not a promise that Joe is personally on every job. The site says so plainly: today Joe runs the crews himself, and as the company grows the standard stays the same. Every job has one named person accountable for it.

## How the services are organized

| Division | What's in it | Who it's for |
|---|---|---|
| **Trees & Grounds** (outside) | Tree removal, trimming, stumps, storm cleanup, lot clearing, landscaping, grounds maintenance | Homeowners, HOAs, builders, landscape companies |
| **Build & Repair** (inside & out) | Remodeling, drywall, paint, flooring, decks, handyman; licensed partners for roofing, electrical, plumbing and HVAC | Homeowners, property managers |
| **Facilities & Contracts** (commercial) | Make-readies, janitorial, pressure washing, grounds contracts, subcontract crews, government work | Property managers, GCs, landscape companies, agencies |
| **Real Estate** (separate division) | Joe is a licensed Texas real estate agent | Homeowners buying or selling |

## Where real estate fits

Real estate is **one more thing the same name looks after, not the headline**.

- On the contracting side (homepage, service pages, tree page), real estate appears **once**, near the bottom, as "Also from Sanches Group." The tree page doesn't mention it at all.
- Each time it appears, it carries the line that work for trade, commercial and property-management customers is never used to source listings. That is what keeps a landscape company or property manager comfortable hiring Sanches Group: Joe will never go around them to their customer.
- On the real estate side (blog posts, /realtor), the bridge runs the other way, and it's natural there: *getting a house ready to sell, or fixing what the inspection found? Joe's crews do that too.*

## Pitching a business (landscape company, GC, property manager)

Lead with the division they need and the vendor promises:

1. We carry our own insurance (COI on request).
2. We work under your name, on your job.
3. We never solicit your customer.
4. One invoice for recurring work.
5. Texas HUB · SDVOSB · SAM.gov, if they have supplier-diversity goals.

Send them `joefsanches.com/trees` for tree work, or the homepage's **Who we work for** section. Don't send them the realtor page.

## Short links to share

- **joefsanches.com**: everything else

## Voice

Plain, direct, first person where it's Joe talking. Promise only what's true now: written quotes, cleanup, one point of contact, insured, veteran-owned. Don't claim certifications or licenses the company doesn't hold. Trade work outside the company's own licenses goes through licensed partners, and the site says so.
